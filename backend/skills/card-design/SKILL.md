# Card Design

Guidance for generating trading card content.

## Stats and cost

A card's power is attack plus defense. The cost-power ratio is power
divided by cost. A balanced creature sits around a ratio of 2.5.

- Low-cost cards (1-2): small stats, ratio may run higher.
- Mid-cost cards (3-5): the backbone of the deck, ratio near 2.5.
- High-cost cards (6+): large stats, often a strong ability.

## Rarity

A deck of 20 cards reads well with roughly:

- 9 common
- 4 rare
- 4 epic
- 3 legendary

Higher rarity should mean a more distinctive ability, not simply
bigger numbers.

## Abilities

Abilities should use the deck's keywords and read in one short
sentence. The ability should reflect the card's theme — a card named
for a fast thing should do something fast.

## Flavor

One evocative line. It is the voice of the card, not a stat summary.

## Thematic Subject Authenticity (Avoid Forcing Humans)

- **Food / Culinary Themes**: When generating cards for food, culinary traditions, or cuisine themes (e.g. Vietnamese Street Food, Bakery, Cooking, Night Market), cards should represent authentic food dishes, bowls, ingredients, cookware, or street market stalls (e.g. "Steaming Phở Bò", "Crispy Bánh Mì", "Spicy Chili Broth", "Spring Rolls"). DO NOT make food cards human characters, chefs, or anthropomorphic food-people.
- **Animals / Beasts / Wildlife / Aquatic Creatures**: Animals and wild beasts must be depicted as true animals and wildlife with realistic or majestic beast anatomy (e.g. quadruped beasts, wolves, crabs, eagles, sharks, sea serpents). DO NOT make them half-human, humanoids, anthro, or people wearing animal armor.
- **Spells / Artifacts / Magic**: Depict the elemental energy, magic phenomena, weapon, or artifact itself without unnecessary human figures.
- **Human Characters**: ONLY depict human characters when the card concept is explicitly a human or humanoid role (e.g. Knight, Priestess, Mage, Archer).

## Character Design and Attire

For cards that are genuinely human/humanoid characters: All human characters (especially female characters such as priestesses, maidens, valkyries, nymphs, sirens, queens, and sorceresses) must be depicted with dignified, practical, and modest attire (e.g. full battle armor, noble tunics, flowing robes, practical traveler garments). Strictly avoid skimpy, overly revealing, or sexualized outfits.
